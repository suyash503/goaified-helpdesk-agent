# Code of Conduct and POSH Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## 1. Expected behaviour

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic is not tolerated.

## 2. Prevention of sexual harassment (POSH)

The company follows the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. An Internal Committee (IC) handles complaints. Complaints should be made in writing to the IC within 3 months of the incident; the IC completes its inquiry within 90 days. Retaliation against anyone who complains is itself misconduct.

## 3. Raising a grievance

For non-POSH grievances, speak to your manager or HR Business Partner first. If that does not resolve it, email the grievance committee through the HR portal (People > Grievance). Grievances are acknowledged within 2 working days.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Declare any outside employment, freelance work, or financial interest in a vendor or competitor to HR before starting it. Moonlighting for a competitor is not allowed.

## 5. Gifts

You may accept gifts from business partners only up to a value of INR 2,000. Anything above that must be declined or handed to HR.

## 6. Confidentiality and data protection

Customer and employee personal data (such as phone numbers, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details) may only be accessed for a business need and must never be shared outside approved systems.

## 7. Disciplinary action

Breaches of this code may lead to a warning, suspension or termination, depending on severity, after a fair inquiry.
