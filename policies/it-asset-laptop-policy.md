# IT Asset and Laptop Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: IT.

## 1. Laptop issue

Every employee receives a company laptop on day one: a 14-inch Windows laptop for most roles, or a MacBook for engineering and design roles. Interns get a laptop from the shared pool. The laptop stays company property.

## 2. Refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every **3 years**, or earlier if IT declares them beyond economical repair. You will get an email from IT 30 days before your refresh date.

## 3. Damage, loss and theft

Report a lost or stolen laptop to the IT Service Desk **within 24 hours** and file a police report (FIR) for theft. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Accidental damage is repaired free of charge once a year; a second incident in the same year may be charged to the employee up to INR 10,000.

## 4. Software installation

Only software from the Company Portal app may be installed. For anything else, raise a software request ticket; IT reviews licensing and security within 3 working days. Pirated or cracked software is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Access requests

Access to business applications (for example Salesforce, Jira or the finance system) is requested through an IT Service Desk ticket and must be approved by your manager. Access follows least privilege and is reviewed every quarter.

## 6. Returning assets on exit

All company assets (laptop, charger, ID card, any accessories) must be returned on or before your last working day. The final settlement is held until IT confirms the return.

## 7. Raising an IT ticket

Raise IT tickets on the IT Service Desk portal or call the IT Service Desk on internal extension 4357 (H-E-L-P). Priority P1 (full outage, security incident) is answered within 1 hour; P2 within 4 hours; P3 within 1 working day; P4 within 3 working days.
