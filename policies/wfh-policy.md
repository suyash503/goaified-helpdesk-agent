# Work From Home (Hybrid) Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## 1. Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 30-day onboarding period are eligible for hybrid work. Roles that need physical presence (facilities, front desk, lab operations) are excluded. Interns are eligible after 30 days with their mentor's approval.

## 2. Standard hybrid schedule

Employees may work from home up to **3 days a week** and must be in the office at least **2 days a week**. Each team sets one common "anchor day" when everyone is in the office. Unused WFH days do not roll over to the next week.

## 3. Requesting extended or full-time WFH

Working from home for more than 3 days a week for longer than 2 weeks (for example for medical reasons, caregiving or relocation) needs written approval from your manager and the HR Business Partner. Requests are raised on the HR portal (People > Work Arrangement) and are reviewed within 5 working days. Approved extended WFH is valid for up to 3 months and can be renewed.

## 4. Working hours and availability

When working from home you must be reachable on Slack and by phone between 10:00 and 17:00 IST (core hours). Update your Slack status when you step away for more than 30 minutes.

## 5. Equipment and internet allowance

The company provides a laptop (see the IT Asset and Laptop Policy). Employees on the hybrid schedule receive an internet allowance of **INR 1,000 per month**, paid with salary. Office furniture is not reimbursed.

## 6. Working from another city or country

Working from a different city in India for up to 4 weeks a year is allowed with manager approval. Working from outside India is not allowed without prior written approval from HR and Legal, because of tax and data-protection rules.

## 7. Security when working remotely

Always connect through the company VPN, never use public computers, and lock your screen whenever you step away. Do not work on confidential data in public places such as cafes or trains.
