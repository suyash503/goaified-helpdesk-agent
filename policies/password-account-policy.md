# Password and Account Security Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: IT Security.

## 1. Password requirements

Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and must not contain your name or employee ID. A passphrase of several random words is recommended. You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## 2. Password expiry

Passwords expire every **90 days**. You get a reminder 14 days and 3 days before expiry. An expired password locks you out of SSO until it is reset.

## 3. Resetting a forgotten password

1. Go to the self-service password reset page linked on the SSO login screen ("Forgot password?").
2. Verify your identity with the Microsoft Authenticator app on your registered phone.
3. Choose a new password that meets section 1.

If self-service reset fails, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4357. The desk will verify your identity with your manager before resetting the password. IT staff will **never** ask for your current password.

## 4. Account lockout

Your account locks for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. To unlock it sooner, use self-service reset or call the IT Service Desk.

## 5. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA through the Microsoft Authenticator app is mandatory for all accounts. If you change or lose your phone, raise a P2 ticket so IT can re-register MFA.

## 6. Sharing credentials

Never share your password, MFA codes or one-time PINs with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff. Shared or service accounts must be stored in the company password manager.

## 7. Reporting phishing

Forward suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. If you clicked a link or entered your password on a suspicious page, reset your password immediately and raise a P1 ticket.
