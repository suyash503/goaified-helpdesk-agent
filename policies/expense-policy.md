# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

## 1. Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims on the Finance portal (Expenses > New Claim) within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Attach an itemised receipt for every expense above INR 500. Approved claims are paid with the next salary run.

## 2. Approval

All claims are approved by your reporting manager. Claims above INR 25,000 also need approval from the department head. Finance may ask for more documents before paying.

## 3. Business travel

- Book flights and hotels through the company travel desk at least 7 days before travel.
- Flights: economy class for all employees on trips shorter than 6 hours.
- Hotels: up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night elsewhere.
- Local travel: app-based cabs or auto-rickshaws; keep the trip receipt.

## 4. Meal allowance

On business travel you can claim meals up to **INR 1,500 per day** in metro cities and **INR 1,000 per day** in other cities. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## 5. Client entertainment

Client meals and entertainment need prior approval from your manager and are capped at INR 2,500 per person. Write the client's company name and the purpose on the claim.

## 6. Learning and certifications

Employees can claim up to INR 20,000 per year for courses, books and certification exams related to their role, with manager approval before purchase.

## 7. Non-reimbursable expenses

Personal entertainment, fines and penalties (including traffic fines), upgrades to business class without approval, and expenses without receipts above INR 500 are not reimbursed.
