# Leave Policy

Brightpath Technologies (fictional). Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## 1. Leave types and entitlement

Every full-time employee gets, per calendar year:

- **Earned leave (EL):** 18 days, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service.
- **Casual leave (CL):** 8 days, credited in full on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners after January).
- **Sick leave (SL):** 10 days, credited in full on 1 January.

Interns and contract staff get 1 day of leave per completed month and are not covered by sections 4 and 5.

## 2. Applying for leave

Apply on the HR portal (People > Leave > Apply). Earned leave of 3 or more days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance. Casual leave should be applied for at least 1 working day in advance except in emergencies. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days; if they do not act, the request is auto-approved on the third working day.

## 3. Sick leave and medical certificates

Sick leave can be applied on the day of absence or within 2 working days after returning. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## 4. Carry forward

- Unused **earned leave** carries forward to the next year, up to a maximum balance of **30 days**. Anything above 30 days lapses on 31 December.
- Unused **casual leave does not carry forward**. It lapses on 31 December.
- Unused sick leave lapses on 31 December.

## 5. Encashment

Earned leave can be encashed only at the time of exit (resignation or retirement), up to 30 days, at the basic salary rate. Casual and sick leave are never encashed.

## 6. Leave without pay

If your balance is exhausted, your manager may approve leave without pay (LWP) of up to 10 days a year. LWP beyond 10 days needs HR approval. LWP days reduce salary and are excluded from the earned-leave accrual for that month.

## 7. Public holidays

The company observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 optional (restricted) holidays per year. The list is published on the HR portal every December. A holiday that falls during approved leave is not counted as leave.
